Method

How the analysis actually works.

No black box. Here's the pipeline — and what it can and can't tell you.

01

Collect

Public online conversations about transportation in your city — forums, social, reviews, comments.

02

Filter

Relevance scoring strips noise, spam and off-topic chatter before anything is analysed.

03

Classify

Each post is assigned standardized narratives, one of 12 emotion classes, and a rider persona.

04

Synthesize

Patterns are aggregated into the signal, with evidence counts and verbatim traceability.

⚖️ What this is not. pulsur measures public online conversation — people who chose to post. It is not a representative survey of all residents, and we say so on every deliverable. Use it to find signals and explain why, alongside your ridership and survey data — not instead of them.
