pulsur design system

The intelligence layer, designed to feel effortless.

A B2B SaaS design language built on the Urban Innovate brand — same colours, same components, extended for dashboards, data and AI insight. Clear enough for non-technical teams, polished enough to wow.

Foundations

Brand & voice

pulsur is the data product of Urban Innovate — “the intelligence layer for user insight.” The personality: calm, confident, human. We turn complex signals into plain answers. Never jargon for its own sake.

Clear over clever

Plain-language labels, one idea per screen. If a smart analyst would say it simply, so do we.

Quietly intelligent

The “smart” lives in the data and the subtle glow — not in busy UI. Let whitespace do the talking.

Trust by default

Show the source, the freshness, the confidence. Non-technical users believe what they can verify.
