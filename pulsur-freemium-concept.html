pulsur Free — one Pulse Report, then request access.

A dead-simple free tier: every new user gets one full, genuinely impressive report for their city — then the live product locks. It’s not a free tier; it’s our best salesperson.

👤 For: CEO & leadership🛠 For: Dev team⏱ 3-minute read
The idea in one sentence

Give every new user exactly one full Pulse Report for their city — real data, no credit card — and after that, the live platform locks until they request access.

The flow

How it works — 5 steps

From landing page to qualified lead. Nothing here is complicated to build.

1

Claim

Sign up with a work email, pick a city + focus. One choice, zero friction.

2

Generate

A ~45s live “listening” screen. The wait signals that real machinery ran for them.

3

Report

One complete, beautiful report. Theirs forever — viewable, downloadable, shareable.

4

Lock

Everything live is blurred behind glass. “Your snapshot is aging—the data moved on.”

5

Ask

One CTA everywhere: Request access. Goes straight to the sales pipeline.

The boundary

What’s free vs. what’s locked

The report is full quality — we never water it down. The scarcity is in frequency & freshness, not quality.

Free forever — the report

  • One full Pulse Report for one city
  • Sentiment index, themes, segments, quotes, 1 AI insight
  • Permanent — view, download PDF, share the link
  • Watermarked “Generated with pulsur”

Locked — the live platform

  • Real-time / refreshed data
  • A second report, second city, or re-run
  • Segment breakdowns, rising themes, alerts
  • Full history & unlimited exports
🔑 Locked ≠ invisible. We show the real charts blurred behind frosted glass with a lock and a line of copy. Every blur is a personalised pitch using the user’s own city data. See the mockup →
Why it works

Why this fits pulsur specifically

We’re sales-led and invite-only. This isn’t a self-serve revenue engine — it’s a lead-qualification machine.

🎯

The aha is their city

Impossible to dismiss as a canned demo. Real local data is what makes an exec forward it internally.

📤

Built to be forwarded

The champion (an analyst) carries the watermarked report to the buyer (a director). The report does the selling.

⏳

Aging is the pitch

A dated snapshot gets staler daily while the live dashboard hums behind glass. “The data moved on” writes itself.

Guardrails

The rules — keep it strict

Strictness is the point. Generosity here just lets low-urgency users camp forever.

1 / org

One report per organization (email domain), not per user — blocks the second-account loophole.

No re-run

No refresh, no second city, no exceptions in-product. Exceptions become a sales tool — reps can gift “one more report” in outreach.

Full quality

The one report is never degraded. One flagship report converts better than three watered-down ones.

For the dev team

What we’d actually build

Small surface area. Most of it reuses the existing report + dashboard we already have.

  • Signup with work email + city/focus picker (one screen).
  • A reports_generated flag keyed to email domain (1 max) — the whole gate lives here.
  • Reuse the existing report generator; add the ~45s “generating” progress screen for theatre.
  • A locked state for the dashboard: same components, rendered blurred + a veil overlay (front-end only).
  • A Request access form → writes a lead (city, focus, email) to the sales pipeline / CRM.
  • One automated email at day ~14: “your report is aging” + a single fresh teaser stat.
How we’ll know it works

Success metrics

We’re optimising for qualified hand-raises, not signups.

Report generated

Signup → report completed. Did they reach the aha?

Shares & PDF downloads

Champion behaviour — our strongest buy signal.

Request-access rate

Report → “request access,” plus revisits to the locked screen.
